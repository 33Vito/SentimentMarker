03 · Evidence and text alignment

Text alignment uses word overlap (Jaccard ≥ 0.38), independently of sentiment. It greedily pairs similar sentences once each; a pair is a reading aid, not proof that one sentence replaced another. Unmatched means no qualifying pair, not necessarily added or deleted policy.

Method and source quality

Topic membership uses explicit keyword matching; each sentence contributes equally. A sentence can count in multiple topics. Means and changes are withheld below five matching sentences. A change is right minus left; positive means more positive dictionary language. Model version and profile must match before a numerical change is shown. These descriptive samples have no confidence interval or significance claim.

The Assessment narrative and substantive headings are included in every edition. Section labels, endnotes, publication metadata and standalone punctuation are excluded from sentence counts. PDF extraction, differing section lengths and topic composition can affect comparisons. The 2025 heading ellipses are retained in the source text. The October 2026 sentence interrupted by the industry callout is rejoined before scoring. Word scores are effective lexicon weights before sentence normalisation; they do not sum directly to the displayed −1…+1 sentence score.
