# Launch week review

The launch went better than we feared. Early users loved the new highlighting, and several wrote that it was the most useful feature we have shipped. The team was proud, and the mood on Monday was excellent.

Then things got difficult. A serious bug corrupted saved highlights for about two hundred customers, and the support queue filled with angry messages. We apologised quickly, but some customers were still frustrated and a few said they would cancel.

## What we learned

The fix was simple, but finding it was painful. Our tests were weak and our monitoring was poor. We now have better alerts, and the team is confident this will not happen again.

Funding is not a worry this quarter. Even so, the runway question is a real concern, and we should not ignore it.
